Workshop Resources

This page contains the details about the attendees of the workshop, including the keynote and panel. The keynote and panel was focused around the 3 challenges and goals of the workshop.

Keynote and Panel

  • Keynote Speaker: Ana Correia de Barros, Head of Human-Centred Design, Fraunhofer AICOS Portugal Keynote on Co-designing a cyberphysical kit to assess subjective wellbeing: Departing from the example of a project which sought to co-design and pilot a cyberphysical kit to help assess subjective wellbeing among workers in a garment factory, this talk will try to explore the concept of ‘leeway’ in healthcare technology design, through the roles of sub-cultures, of users’ active design engagements, and of technologists’ values in the processes of meaning-making for healthcare technologies.
  • Panelists:
    • Amanda Hall, Senior Researcher, Health Futures Group, Microsoft Research
    • Dong Whi Yoo, Assistant Professor, Human-Centered Computing, Indiana University Indianapolis
    • Jessica Pater, Senior Research Scientist & Manager, Health Services and Informatics Research, Parkview Research Center
    • Pedro Reis Pereira, Nephrology, Hospital Da Luz
